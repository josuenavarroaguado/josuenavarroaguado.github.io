Frequently asked

A first framework for understanding the fit.

Do you work with startups?

Yes, especially when there is already a product, users, and a technology team, and architecture or engineering decisions are becoming strategically important.

Can you work alongside an existing CTO?

Yes. Architecture Advisory or Strategic Technology Advisor engagements can provide an independent second opinion or focused support during a transformation.

Do you recommend microservices?

Only when they solve a real problem. In many contexts, a well-designed modular architecture is a better decision.

What is the first step?

An initial conversation to understand the challenge, business context, and current technology state.
